Slidey Slides Park

Vancouver · Free · 1 hr · Outdoor

Slidey Slides Park is a park in Vancouver that is free to visit, and families usually spend about 1 hr here.

Playground with washrooms.

It is outdoors, so it is best on a dry day; bring layers. It suits babies and toddlers (0–3), preschoolers (3–5) and school-age kids (5–12).

Hours: 06:00-22:00.
