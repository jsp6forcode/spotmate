Albion Sports Complex

Maple Ridge · Free · 1–2 hrs · Both

Albion Sports Complex is a rec centre in Maple Ridge that is free to visit, and families usually spend about 1–2 hrs here.

Recreation centre with a playground, a spray park and sports fields.

It is mostly outdoors but still workable in light rain. It suits babies and toddlers (0–3), preschoolers (3–5) and school-age kids (5–12).
