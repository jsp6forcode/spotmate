Places to take kids in North Vancouver

82 kid-friendly places in North Vancouver: places picked for families with babies, toddlers and school-age kids. 58 free, 21 indoor. Open any spot in Tiny Trips for hours and what parents say.

Planning a day out in North Vancouver with kids? These 82 places cover playgrounds, parks, indoor play and more, including Capilano River Regional Park, Cates Park (Whey-ah-Wichen), Lynn Headwaters Regional Park and Quarry Rock.

Standouts worth a special trip: Capilano River Regional Park, Cates Park (Whey-ah-Wichen), Lynn Headwaters Regional Park and Quarry Rock.

Quick answers

Which places in North Vancouver are worth a special trip?

Tiny Trips marks Capilano River Regional Park, Cates Park (Whey-ah-Wichen), Lynn Headwaters Regional Park and Quarry Rock as standout spots.

Are there free places in North Vancouver?

Yes. 58 of the 82 are free, including Capilano River Regional Park, Cates Park (Whey-ah-Wichen), Lynn Headwaters Regional Park, Quarry Rock and 30 Foot Pool.

What is good on a rainy day in North Vancouver?

Indoor options on this list: Canlan Sports North Shore, Capilano Mall, Climb Base5, Delbrook Community Recreation Centre and Happy Claw.

How long should we plan for?

Most visits here take about 1–2 hrs. Each listing shows its own time.
