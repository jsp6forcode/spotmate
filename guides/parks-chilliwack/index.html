Parks in Chilliwack for kids

32 kid-friendly places in Chilliwack: parks picked for families with babies, toddlers and school-age kids. 32 free. Open any spot in Tiny Trips for hours and what parents say.

Looking for parks in Chilliwack? Tiny Trips lists 32 that parents can actually use with kids, from Barber Park, Bill Kelly Park and Carmel Park to smaller neighbourhood options.

This kind of place suits babies and toddlers (0–3), preschoolers (3–5) and school-age kids (5–12), so check each listing for the best age and how long a visit usually takes.

Quick answers

Are there free parks in Chilliwack?

Yes. All 32 spots on this list are free to visit.

What is good on a rainy day in Chilliwack?

These spots are mostly outdoors. For wet days, open the Indoor filter in the app or see the indoor play guides.

What ages are parks in Chilliwack good for?

They suit babies and toddlers (0–3), preschoolers (3–5) and school-age kids (5–12).

How long should we plan for?

Most visits here take about 1 hr. Each listing shows its own time.
