Playgrounds in Metro Vancouver for kids

480 kid-friendly places across Metro Vancouver: playgrounds picked for families with babies, toddlers and school-age kids. 464 free. Open any spot in Tiny Trips for hours and what parents say.

Looking for playgrounds across Metro Vancouver? Tiny Trips lists 480 that parents can actually use with kids, from Terra Nova Adventure Play, 125G Neighbourhood Park and 140 Street Playground to smaller neighbourhood options.

This kind of place suits babies and toddlers (0–3), preschoolers (3–5) and school-age kids (5–12), so check each listing for the best age and how long a visit usually takes.

Standouts worth a special trip: Terra Nova Adventure Play.

Quick answers

Which playgrounds across Metro Vancouver are worth a special trip?

Tiny Trips marks Terra Nova Adventure Play as standout spots.

Are there free playgrounds across Metro Vancouver?

Yes. 464 of the 480 are free, including Terra Nova Adventure Play, 125G Neighbourhood Park, 140 Street Playground, 89A Avenue Park and 89A Playground.

What is good on a rainy day across Metro Vancouver?

These spots are mostly outdoors. For wet days, open the Indoor filter in the app or see the indoor play guides.

What ages are playgrounds across Metro Vancouver good for?

They suit babies and toddlers (0–3), preschoolers (3–5) and school-age kids (5–12).

How long should we plan for?

Most visits here take about 1–2 hrs. Each listing shows its own time.
