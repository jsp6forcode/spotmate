Places to take kids in Delta

69 kid-friendly places in Delta: places picked for families with babies, toddlers and school-age kids. 56 free, 12 indoor. Open any spot in Tiny Trips for hours and what parents say.

Quick answers

Which places in Delta are worth a special trip?

Tiny Trips marks Centennial Beach and Watershed Park as standout spots.

Are there free places in Delta?

Yes. 56 of the 69 are free, including Centennial Beach, Watershed Park, 81A Avenue Park, 89A Avenue Park and 89A Playground.

What is good on a rainy day in Delta?

Indoor options on this list: CipherX Surrey Delta, Delta Gymnastics Society, Douglas J. Husband Discovery Centre, George Mackie Library - North Delta and Ladner Community Centre.

How long should we plan for?

Most visits here take about 1–2 hrs. Each listing shows its own time.
