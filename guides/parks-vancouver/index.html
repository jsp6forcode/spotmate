Parks in Vancouver for kids

22 kid-friendly places in Vancouver: parks picked for families with babies, toddlers and school-age kids. 22 free. Open any spot in Tiny Trips for hours, parking and what parents say.

Quick answers

Which parks in Vancouver are worth a special trip?

Tiny Trips marks Queen Elizabeth Park as standout spots.

Are there free parks in Vancouver?

Yes. All 22 spots on this list are free to visit.

What is good on a rainy day in Vancouver?

These spots are mostly outdoors. For wet days, open the Indoor filter in the app or see the indoor play guides.

What ages are parks in Vancouver good for?

They suit babies and toddlers (0–3), preschoolers (3–5) and school-age kids (5–12).

How long should we plan for?

Most visits here take about 1–2 hrs. Each listing shows its own time.
