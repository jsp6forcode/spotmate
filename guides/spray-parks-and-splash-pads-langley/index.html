Spray parks and splash pads in Langley for kids

10 kid-friendly places in Langley: spray parks and splash pads picked for families with babies, toddlers and school-age kids. 8 free. Open any spot in Tiny Trips for hours, parking and what parents say.

Quick answers

Are there free spray parks and splash pads in Langley?

Yes. 8 of the 10 are free, including Brookswood Park, Douglas Park, Fort Langley Park, Philip Jackman Park and The Outdoor Experience.

What is good on a rainy day in Langley?

These spots are mostly outdoors. For wet days, open the Indoor filter in the app or see the indoor play guides.

What ages are spray parks and splash pads in Langley good for?

They suit babies and toddlers (0–3), preschoolers (3–5) and school-age kids (5–12).

How long should we plan for?

Most visits here take about 1–2 hrs. Each listing shows its own time.
