Rec centres in Metro Vancouver for kids

95 kid-friendly places across Metro Vancouver: rec centres picked for families with babies, toddlers and school-age kids. 18 free, 66 indoor. Open any spot in Tiny Trips for hours, parking and what parents say.

Quick answers

Are there free rec centres across Metro Vancouver?

Yes. 18 of the 95 are free, including Albion Sports Complex, Aldergrove Kinsmen Community Centre, Brentwood Community Resource Centre, Cambie Community Centre and Chuck Bailey Recreation Centre.

What is good on a rainy day across Metro Vancouver?

Indoor options on this list: Abbotsford Recreation Centre, Albion Community Centre, Bob Chan-Kent Family YMCA, Champlain Heights Community Centre and Cheam Leisure Centre.

What ages are rec centres across Metro Vancouver good for?

They suit babies and toddlers (0–3), preschoolers (3–5) and school-age kids (5–12).

How long should we plan for?

Most visits here take about 1–2 hrs. Each listing shows its own time.
