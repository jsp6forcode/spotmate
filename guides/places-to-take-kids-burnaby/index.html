Places to take kids in Burnaby

62 kid-friendly places in Burnaby: places picked for families with babies, toddlers and school-age kids. 48 free, 16 indoor. Open any spot in Tiny Trips for hours and what parents say.

Quick answers

Which places in Burnaby are worth a special trip?

Tiny Trips marks Barnet Marine Park, Central Park and Deer Lake Park as standout spots.

Are there free places in Burnaby?

Yes. 48 of the 62 are free, including Barnet Marine Park, Central Park, Deer Lake Park, Avondale Park and Brentwood Community Resource Centre.

What is good on a rainy day in Burnaby?

Indoor options on this list: Burnaby Public Library, Bob Prittie Metrotown Branch, Christine Sinclair Community Centre, Clawzone Arcade, Confederation Community Centre and Edmonds Community Centre.

How long should we plan for?

Most visits here take about 1–2 hrs. Each listing shows its own time.
