Arts and crafts for kids in Metro Vancouver

12 kid-friendly places across Metro Vancouver: arts and crafts picked for families with babies, toddlers and school-age kids. 2 free, 12 indoor. Open any spot in Tiny Trips for hours, parking and what parents say.

Quick answers

Are there free arts and crafts across Metro Vancouver?

Yes. 2 of the 12 are free, including PoMoArts and Surrey Art Gallery & Surrey Civic Theatres at Surrey Arts Centre.

What is good on a rainy day across Metro Vancouver?

All 12 spots on this list are indoors, so they work in any weather.

What ages are arts and crafts across Metro Vancouver good for?

They suit preschoolers (3–5) and school-age kids (5–12).

How long should we plan for?

Most visits here take about 1–2 hrs. Each listing shows its own time.
