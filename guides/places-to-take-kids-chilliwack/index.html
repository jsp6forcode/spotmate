Places to take kids in Chilliwack

50 kid-friendly places in Chilliwack: places picked for families with babies, toddlers and school-age kids. 41 free, 11 indoor. Open any spot in Tiny Trips for hours and what parents say.

Planning a day out in Chilliwack with kids? These 50 places cover playgrounds, parks, indoor play and more, including Barber Park, Bill Kelly Park, Bob Chan-Kent Family YMCA and Carmel Park.

Quick answers

Are there free places in Chilliwack?

Yes. 41 of the 50 are free, including Barber Park, Bill Kelly Park, Carmel Park, Cedarbrook playground and Central Community Park.

What is good on a rainy day in Chilliwack?

Indoor options on this list: Bob Chan-Kent Family YMCA, Cheam Leisure Centre, Chilliwack Landing Leisure Centre, Chilliwack Library and Chilliwack Museum.

How long should we plan for?

Most visits here take about 1–2 hrs. Each listing shows its own time.
