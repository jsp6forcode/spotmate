Places to take kids in New Westminster

41 kid-friendly places in New Westminster: places picked for families with babies, toddlers and school-age kids. 33 free, 13 indoor. Open any spot in Tiny Trips for hours, parking and what parents say.

Quick answers

Which places in New Westminster are worth a special trip?

Tiny Trips marks Queen's Park and Waterfront Esplanade as standout spots.

Are there free places in New Westminster?

Yes. 33 of the 41 are free, including Queen's Park, Waterfront Esplanade, Albert Crescent Park, Baby Nook and Cariboo Park.

What is good on a rainy day in New Westminster?

Indoor options on this list: Baby Nook, Braid St Art Studios & Gallery, Extreme Air Park, Fraser River Discovery Centre and HANABaby Adventure World.

How long should we plan for?

Most visits here take about 1–2 hrs. Each listing shows its own time.
