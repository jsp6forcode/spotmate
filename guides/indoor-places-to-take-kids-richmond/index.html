Indoor places to take kids in Richmond

28 kid-friendly places in Richmond: indoor places picked for families with babies, toddlers and school-age kids. 8 free, 28 indoor. Open any spot in Tiny Trips for hours, parking and what parents say.

Quick answers

Are there free indoor places in Richmond?

Yes. 8 of the 28 are free, including Britannia Shipyards National Historic Site, CF Richmond Centre, IKEA Richmond, Lansdowne Centre and Richmond Nature House.

What is good on a rainy day in Richmond?

All 28 spots on this list are indoors, so they work in any weather.

How long should we plan for?

Most visits here take about 1–2 hrs. Each listing shows its own time.
