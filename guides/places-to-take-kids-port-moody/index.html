Places to take kids in Port Moody

32 kid-friendly places in Port Moody: places picked for families with babies, toddlers and school-age kids. 29 free, 7 indoor. Open any spot in Tiny Trips for hours, parking and what parents say.

Quick answers

Which places in Port Moody are worth a special trip?

Tiny Trips marks Rocky Point Park and Sasamat Lake as standout spots.

Are there free places in Port Moody?

Yes. 29 of the 32 are free, including Rocky Point Park, Sasamat Lake, Admiralty Point Park, Ailsa Park and Art Wilkinson Park.

What is good on a rainy day in Port Moody?

Indoor options on this list: Glenayre Community Centre, Inlet Theatre, Kyle Centre, Little Beans Play Cafe and POMO Museum.

How long should we plan for?

Most visits here take about 1–2 hrs. Each listing shows its own time.
