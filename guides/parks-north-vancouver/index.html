Parks in North Vancouver for kids

32 kid-friendly places in North Vancouver: parks picked for families with babies, toddlers and school-age kids. 32 free. Open any spot in Tiny Trips for hours and what parents say.

Looking for parks in North Vancouver? Tiny Trips lists 32 that parents can actually use with kids, from Capilano River Regional Park, Cates Park (Whey-ah-Wichen) and Lynn Headwaters Regional Park to smaller neighbourhood options.

This kind of place suits babies and toddlers (0–3), preschoolers (3–5) and school-age kids (5–12), so check each listing for the best age and how long a visit usually takes.

Standouts worth a special trip: Capilano River Regional Park, Cates Park (Whey-ah-Wichen) and Lynn Headwaters Regional Park.

Quick answers

Which parks in North Vancouver are worth a special trip?

Tiny Trips marks Capilano River Regional Park, Cates Park (Whey-ah-Wichen) and Lynn Headwaters Regional Park as standout spots.

Are there free parks in North Vancouver?

Yes. All 32 spots on this list are free to visit.

What is good on a rainy day in North Vancouver?

These spots are mostly outdoors. For wet days, open the Indoor filter in the app or see the indoor play guides.

What ages are parks in North Vancouver good for?

They suit babies and toddlers (0–3), preschoolers (3–5) and school-age kids (5–12).

How long should we plan for?

Most visits here take about 1–2 hrs. Each listing shows its own time.
