Places to take kids in Langley

61 kid-friendly places in Langley: places picked for families with babies, toddlers and school-age kids. 39 free, 16 indoor. Open any spot in Tiny Trips for hours and what parents say.

Quick answers

Which places in Langley are worth a special trip?

Tiny Trips marks Campbell Valley Park, Derby Reach Park and Sendall Gardens as standout spots.

Are there free places in Langley?

Yes. 39 of the 61 are free, including Campbell Valley Park, Derby Reach Park, Sendall Gardens, Aldergrove Kinsmen Community Centre and Aldergrove Regional Park.

What is good on a rainy day in Langley?

Indoor options on this list: Activate Langley, Canadian Museum of Flight, Chuck E. Cheese, City of Langley Library and Douglas Recreation Centre.

How long should we plan for?

Most visits here take about 1–2 hrs. Each listing shows its own time.
