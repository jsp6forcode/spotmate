Indoor places to take kids in Chilliwack

11 indoor places to take kids in Chilliwack, good for rainy days: play centres, libraries, museums, pools and more for babies, toddlers and school-age kids. 4 of them free. Open any spot in Tiny Trips for hours and what parents say.

When it rains, is too hot or too cold, these 11 indoor places in Chilliwack keep kids busy: Bob Chan-Kent Family YMCA, Cheam Leisure Centre, Chilliwack Landing Leisure Centre and Chilliwack Library and more. Each one is checked for kids, with the hours and what parents say inside the app.

Quick answers

Are there free indoor places in Chilliwack?

Yes. 4 of the 11 are free, including Chilliwack Library, Chilliwack Museum, Cottonwood Centre and Sardis Library.

What is good on a rainy day in Chilliwack?

All 11 spots on this list are indoors, so they work in any weather.

How long should we plan for?

Most visits here take about 1–2 hrs. Each listing shows its own time.
