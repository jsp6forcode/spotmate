Playgrounds in New Westminster for kids

27 kid-friendly places in New Westminster: playgrounds picked for families with babies, toddlers and school-age kids. 27 free. Open any spot in Tiny Trips for hours and what parents say.

Looking for playgrounds in New Westminster? Tiny Trips lists 27 that parents can actually use with kids, from Albert Crescent Park, Cariboo Park and Connaught Heights Park to smaller neighbourhood options.

This kind of place suits babies and toddlers (0–3), preschoolers (3–5) and school-age kids (5–12), so check each listing for the best age and how long a visit usually takes.

Quick answers

Are there free playgrounds in New Westminster?

Yes. All 27 spots on this list are free to visit.

What is good on a rainy day in New Westminster?

These spots are mostly outdoors. For wet days, open the Indoor filter in the app or see the indoor play guides.

What ages are playgrounds in New Westminster good for?

They suit babies and toddlers (0–3), preschoolers (3–5) and school-age kids (5–12).

How long should we plan for?

Most visits here take about 1–2 hrs. Each listing shows its own time.
