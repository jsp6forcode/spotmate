Places to take kids in Vancouver

93 kid-friendly places in Vancouver: places picked for families with babies, toddlers and school-age kids. 50 free, 42 indoor. Open any spot in Tiny Trips for hours, parking and what parents say.

Quick answers

Which places in Vancouver are worth a special trip?

Tiny Trips marks Kitsilano Beach, Museum of Anthropology, Nitobe Memorial Garden and Quarry Rock as standout spots.

Are there free places in Vancouver?

Yes. 50 of the 93 are free, including Kitsilano Beach, Quarry Rock, Queen Elizabeth Park, Spanish Banks and Whytecliff Park.

What is good on a rainy day in Vancouver?

Indoor options on this list: Museum of Anthropology, Beaty Biodiversity Museum, Bloedel Conservatory, Champlain Heights Community Centre and Creekside Community Recreation Centre.

How long should we plan for?

Most visits here take about 1–2 hrs. Each listing shows its own time.
