Places to take kids in Surrey

78 kid-friendly places in Surrey: places picked for families with babies, toddlers and school-age kids. 50 free, 33 indoor. Open any spot in Tiny Trips for hours and what parents say.

Quick answers

Which places in Surrey are worth a special trip?

Tiny Trips marks Bear Creek Park, Blackie Spit, Green Timbers Urban Forest and Redwood Park as standout spots.

Are there free places in Surrey?

Yes. 50 of the 78 are free, including Bear Creek Park, Blackie Spit, Green Timbers Urban Forest, Redwood Park and Tynehead Regional Park.

What is good on a rainy day in Surrey?

Indoor options on this list: Central City Shopping Centre, Clayton Community Centre, Cloverdale Arena, Cloverdale Recreation Centre and Cloverdale Sport & Ice Complex.

How long should we plan for?

Most visits here take about 1–2 hrs. Each listing shows its own time.
