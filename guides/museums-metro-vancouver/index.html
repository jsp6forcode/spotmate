Museums for kids in Metro Vancouver

33 kid-friendly places across Metro Vancouver: museums picked for families with babies, toddlers and school-age kids. 14 free, 32 indoor. Open any spot in Tiny Trips for hours, parking and what parents say.

Quick answers

Which museums across Metro Vancouver are worth a special trip?

Tiny Trips marks Burnaby Village Museum and Museum of Anthropology as standout spots.

Are there free museums across Metro Vancouver?

Yes. 14 of the 33 are free, including Burnaby Village Museum, Britannia Shipyards National Historic Site, Chilliwack Museum, Mennonite Heritage Museum and Museum of Archaeology & Ethnology.

What is good on a rainy day across Metro Vancouver?

Indoor options on this list: Museum of Anthropology, Beaty Biodiversity Museum, Britannia Shipyards National Historic Site, Canadian Museum of Flight and Chilliwack Museum.

What ages are museums across Metro Vancouver good for?

They suit preschoolers (3–5) and school-age kids (5–12).

How long should we plan for?

Most visits here take about 1–2 hrs. Each listing shows its own time.
