Parks for kids in Metro Vancouver

391 kid-friendly places across Metro Vancouver: parks picked for families with babies, toddlers and school-age kids. 390 free. Open any spot in Tiny Trips for hours, parking and what parents say.

Quick answers

Which parks across Metro Vancouver are worth a special trip?

Tiny Trips marks Barnet Marine Park, Bear Creek Park, Campbell Valley Park and Capilano River Regional Park as standout spots.

Are there free parks across Metro Vancouver?

Yes. 390 of the 391 are free, including Barnet Marine Park, Bear Creek Park, Campbell Valley Park, Capilano River Regional Park and Cates Park (Whey-ah-Wichen).

What is good on a rainy day across Metro Vancouver?

These spots are mostly outdoors. For wet days, open the Indoor filter in the app or see the indoor play guides.

What ages are parks across Metro Vancouver good for?

They suit babies and toddlers (0–3), preschoolers (3–5) and school-age kids (5–12).

How long should we plan for?

Most visits here take about 1–2 hrs. Each listing shows its own time.
