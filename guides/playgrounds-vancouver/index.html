Playgrounds in Vancouver for kids

53 kid-friendly places in Vancouver: playgrounds picked for families with babies, toddlers and school-age kids. 49 free. Open any spot in Tiny Trips for hours and what parents say.

Looking for playgrounds in Vancouver? Tiny Trips lists 53 that parents can actually use with kids, from Aberdeen Park Playground, Aberthau Playground and Acadia Park Playground to smaller neighbourhood options.

This kind of place suits babies and toddlers (0–3), preschoolers (3–5) and school-age kids (5–12), so check each listing for the best age and how long a visit usually takes.

Quick answers

Are there free playgrounds in Vancouver?

Yes. 49 of the 53 are free, including Aberdeen Park Playground, Aberthau Playground, Acadia Park Playground, Alexandra Park and Andy Livingstone Park.

What is good on a rainy day in Vancouver?

These spots are mostly outdoors. For wet days, open the Indoor filter in the app or see the indoor play guides.

What ages are playgrounds in Vancouver good for?

They suit babies and toddlers (0–3), preschoolers (3–5) and school-age kids (5–12).

How long should we plan for?

Most visits here take about 1 hr. Each listing shows its own time.
