Places to take kids in Richmond

57 kid-friendly places in Richmond: places picked for families with babies, toddlers and school-age kids. 33 free, 28 indoor. Open any spot in Tiny Trips for hours, parking and what parents say.

Quick answers

Which places in Richmond are worth a special trip?

Tiny Trips marks Garry Point Park, Iona Beach Regional Park and Terra Nova Adventure Play as standout spots.

Are there free places in Richmond?

Yes. 33 of the 57 are free, including Garry Point Park, Iona Beach Regional Park, Terra Nova Adventure Play, Aberdeen Neighbourhood Park and Britannia Shipyards National Historic Site.

What is good on a rainy day in Richmond?

Indoor options on this list: 365Fundays family fun centre, Britannia Shipyards National Historic Site, CF Richmond Centre, City Centre Community Centre and Clip 'n Climb Vancouver.

How long should we plan for?

Most visits here take about 1–2 hrs. Each listing shows its own time.
