Places to take kids in Coquitlam

57 kid-friendly places in Coquitlam: places picked for families with babies, toddlers and school-age kids. 42 free, 17 indoor. Open any spot in Tiny Trips for hours and what parents say.

Quick answers

Which places in Coquitlam are worth a special trip?

Tiny Trips marks Como Lake Park, Coquitlam River Park, Minnekhada Regional Park and Mundy Park as standout spots.

Are there free places in Coquitlam?

Yes. 42 of the 57 are free, including Como Lake Park, Coquitlam River Park, Minnekhada Regional Park, Mundy Park and Town Centre Park & Lafarge Lake.

What is good on a rainy day in Coquitlam?

Indoor options on this list: CipherX Coquitlam | Escape Room & VR Lounge, Club Aviva Gymnastics, Coquitlam Centre, Coquitlam Public Library, Poirier Branch and Crash Crawly’s.

How long should we plan for?

Most visits here take about 1–2 hrs. Each listing shows its own time.
