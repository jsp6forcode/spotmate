Parks in Maple Ridge for kids

36 kid-friendly places in Maple Ridge: parks picked for families with babies, toddlers and school-age kids. 36 free. Open any spot in Tiny Trips for hours and what parents say.

Looking for parks in Maple Ridge? Tiny Trips lists 36 that parents can actually use with kids, from Golden Ears Park, Kanaka Creek Regional Park and Albion Park to smaller neighbourhood options.

This kind of place suits babies and toddlers (0–3), preschoolers (3–5) and school-age kids (5–12), so check each listing for the best age and how long a visit usually takes.

Standouts worth a special trip: Golden Ears Park and Kanaka Creek Regional Park.

Quick answers

Which parks in Maple Ridge are worth a special trip?

Tiny Trips marks Golden Ears Park and Kanaka Creek Regional Park as standout spots.

Are there free parks in Maple Ridge?

Yes. All 36 spots on this list are free to visit.

What is good on a rainy day in Maple Ridge?

These spots are mostly outdoors. For wet days, open the Indoor filter in the app or see the indoor play guides.

What ages are parks in Maple Ridge good for?

They suit babies and toddlers (0–3), preschoolers (3–5) and school-age kids (5–12).

How long should we plan for?

Most visits here take about 1 hr. Each listing shows its own time.
