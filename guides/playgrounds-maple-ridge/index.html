Playgrounds in Maple Ridge for kids

31 kid-friendly places in Maple Ridge: playgrounds picked for families with babies, toddlers and school-age kids. 31 free. Open any spot in Tiny Trips for hours and what parents say.

Quick answers

Are there free playgrounds in Maple Ridge?

Yes. All 31 spots on this list are free to visit.

What is good on a rainy day in Maple Ridge?

These spots are mostly outdoors. For wet days, open the Indoor filter in the app or see the indoor play guides.

What ages are playgrounds in Maple Ridge good for?

They suit babies and toddlers (0–3), preschoolers (3–5) and school-age kids (5–12).

How long should we plan for?

Most visits here take about 1 hr. Each listing shows its own time.
