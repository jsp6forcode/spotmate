Parks in Coquitlam for kids

34 kid-friendly places in Coquitlam: parks picked for families with babies, toddlers and school-age kids. 34 free. Open any place in Tiny Trips for hours and what parents say.

Looking for parks in Coquitlam? Tiny Trips lists 34 that parents can actually use with kids, from Coquitlam River Park, Minnekhada Regional Park and Mundy Park to smaller neighbourhood options. They are spread across Coquitlam, Burke Mountain, Coquitlam and Coquitlam (Burke Mountain), so there is usually one close to home.

This kind of place suits babies and toddlers (0–3), preschoolers (3–5) and school-age kids (5–12), so check each listing for the best age and how long a visit usually takes.

Standouts worth a special trip: Coquitlam River Park, Minnekhada Regional Park and Mundy Park.

Quick answers

Which parks in Coquitlam are worth a special trip?

Tiny Trips marks Coquitlam River Park, Minnekhada Regional Park and Mundy Park as standout places.

Are there free parks in Coquitlam?

Yes. All 34 places on this list are free to visit.

What is good on a rainy day in Coquitlam?

These places are mostly outdoors. For wet days, open the Indoor filter in the app or see the indoor play guides.

What ages are parks in Coquitlam good for?

They suit babies and toddlers (0–3), preschoolers (3–5) and school-age kids (5–12).

How long should we plan for?

Most visits here take about 1–2 hrs. Each listing shows its own time.
