Indoor places to take kids in Metro Vancouver

250 indoor places to take kids across Metro Vancouver, good for rainy days: play centres, libraries, museums, pools and more for babies, toddlers and school-age kids. 73 of them free. Open any spot in Tiny Trips for hours, parking and what parents say.

Are there free indoor places across Metro Vancouver?

Yes. 73 of the 250 are free, including Abbotsford Community Library, Baby Nook, Britannia Shipyards National Historic Site, Burnaby Public Library, Bob Prittie Metrotown Branch and Capilano Mall.

What is good on a rainy day across Metro Vancouver?

All 250 spots on this list are indoors, so they work in any weather.

Most visits here take about 1–2 hrs. Each listing shows its own time.
